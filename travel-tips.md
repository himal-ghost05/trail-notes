# Travel Packing Essentials

Use this checklist as a starting point and adapt it to the weather, route, and length of your trip. For hikes, check local conditions and carry the essentials required for your area.

## Clothing
- Comfortable, broken-in walking or hiking shoes
- Moisture-wicking base layers and spare socks
- Weather-appropriate layers, including a warm layer
- Waterproof jacket and trousers when rain is possible
- Hat or cap, sunglasses, and gloves as conditions require

## Navigation and safety
- Phone with offline maps downloaded
- Paper map and compass for remote routes
- Headlamp or flashlight with spare batteries
- First-aid kit and any personal medication
- Whistle, emergency blanket, and a small repair kit
- Power bank and charging cable
- Share your route and expected return time with someone

## Water and food
- Reusable water bottles or a hydration reservoir
- Water treatment method for routes without reliable refill points
- More snacks and food than you expect to need
- Reusable utensils and a small bag for rubbish

## Personal essentials
- ID, payment card, and a little cash
- Toiletries, sunscreen, and insect repellent
- Hand sanitizer and tissues
- Reservation details, tickets, and emergency contacts

## Before leaving
- Check the forecast, daylight hours, and trail notices
- Charge devices and test essential gear
- Pack heavier items close to your back and keep rain gear accessible
- Leave no trace: pack out everything you bring in
